# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

An interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing retail sales, profitability, product performance, customer contributions, and sales trends across the United States from 2014 to 2017.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw retail sales data into an interactive dashboard using Microsoft Excel. It enables users to explore business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), PivotTables, PivotCharts, and interactive slicers.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales trends and profitability to support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total sales, total profit, and total sales records.
- Analyze product category and sub-category performance.
- Compare yearly profit trends across categories.
- Identify monthly sales patterns and peak periods.
- Evaluate state-wise sales performance.
- Identify customers generating the highest profit.
- Enable interactive analysis using Category and Year slicers.

## 📂 Dataset

- **File:** `salesdata.csv`
- **Period:** 2014–2017
- **Records:** Approximately 9,993
- **Coverage:** Retail sales across multiple US states
- **Key Fields:** Order Date, Customer Name, State, Category, Sales, Quantity, and Profit

**Data Note:** The dataset does not contain an Order ID field. Therefore, 9,993 represents the number of sales records, not unique orders.

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

- KPI cards displaying key sales metrics.
- Category and Year slicers for interactive exploration.
- Product sub-category sales comparison.
- Yearly profit trend analysis.
- Monthly sales trend visualization.
- State-wise sales analysis.
- Customer profitability analysis.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- **Product Performance:** Phones lead sales in Technology, Chairs lead sales in Furniture, and Storage leads sales in Office Supplies.
- **Profitability:** The dashboard enables comparison of profit trends across categories and years.
- **Seasonality:** Monthly analysis helps identify periods of higher sales.
- **Geographical Performance:** State-level analysis highlights differences in sales performance.
- **Customer Analysis:** Customer-level profit comparison identifies high-performing customers.

*Note: Insights should be interpreted using the final dashboard and its selected filters.*

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Review product costs, pricing, and discounts to improve profitability.
- Plan inventory and staffing according to seasonal sales patterns.
- Investigate state-level differences to identify growth opportunities.
- Develop retention strategies for high-profit customers.
- Monitor category-level profit trends to support business planning.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The dataset covers only the 2014–2017 period.
- Unique orders cannot be identified without an Order ID.
- The project focuses on historical descriptive analysis and does not include predictive forecasting.
